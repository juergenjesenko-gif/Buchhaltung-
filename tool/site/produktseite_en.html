How it works

From till receipt to tidy books

  1. Snap the receipt

    After the shop, when you open the post or after an online order: point your phone at it, done.

  2. Check and save

    Amount, VAT rate, date and supplier are read and suggested (Pro). You take a quick look and save with one tap. The app keeps the receipt.

  3. Stay on top of it

    Income, spending, unpaid invoices and upcoming dates at a glance. At year end you hand everything over to your accountant in one export.

What the app does

Your office in one app

Capture receipts PRO

Till receipts, bills and PDFs are read for you. You check the suggestion and save with one tap. Entering by hand works on every plan.

Quotes and invoices

Create them on your phone and share them as a PDF. Mistakes are corrected with a cancellation, so your records stay clean.

Keep contracts

Store contracts and keep an eye on notice periods. A short summary via text recognition in Pro; regular payments are suggested as entries.

Year overview for your accountant

Totals by category and a list of your equipment, ready to export. Your accountant takes it from there.

Dates at a glance

Upcoming deadlines and your figures for the year on one screen, so nothing sneaks up on you.

Your data stays with you

Your books live on your phone, not on our servers. Backups are encrypted with your password; the app can be locked with a PIN, fingerprint or Face ID.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is JeNi a real person?

No. JeNi is an AI-generated character from JeNi books, and her roles on Instagram are fictional. Replies from @jeni.books.app in comments and messages are written by AI. The app behind her is real.

Can I use JeNi books in the UK today?

Not yet. The app is currently available in German for sole traders in Austria and Germany. The English version for the UK is in preparation. Join the waiting list and we will tell you when it is ready.

Does the app replace my accountant?

No. The app does the sums and gets things ready, but it does not give tax advice. It takes the collecting, entering and filing off your hands, so your accountant gets everything complete.

Will it work with Making Tax Digital?

We are working on the UK version and will only say more once it is built and checked. Until then, please do not rely on JeNi books for anything you have to send to HMRC.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. There is no user account and no server holding your books. Backups go where you choose and are encrypted with your password.

What will it cost?

UK prices in pounds will be announced before launch, together with the free trial and all terms.
